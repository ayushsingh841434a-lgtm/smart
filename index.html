From walking in to marked present — four steps

The same flow every session, whether it's a 40-seat lab or a 300-seat lecture hall.

Step 1

Open Mark Attendance

Student opens the camera screen for the current class or subject session.

Step 2

Face detected

A bounding box locks onto the face and confirms good lighting and framing.

Step 3

Identity verified

The captured face is matched against the student's registered face template.

Step 4

Attendance marked

Name, ID, subject, date and time are logged instantly — duplicates are blocked.
